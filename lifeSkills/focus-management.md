## Question 1

What is Deep Work?
- Deep Work means focusing on a difficult and important task without getting distracted. In other words, it is a state where we give our full attention to one task and use our brain to its maximum potential.
## Question 2

According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?
- Choose an important task that requires your full concentration.
- Find a quiet place to work and keep yourself away from distractions.
- Work on one task at a time instead of multitasking.

## Question 3

How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?

- I can keep my phone and social media away while working and choose a quiet place so that I can concentrate better.
- I can focus on one important task at a time instead of multitasking.
- I can set clear goals for what I want to complete during my deep work session.

## Question 4

What are the dangers of social media, in brief?
- Social media can become addictive and make us constantly check our phones.
- It can reduce our ability to focus for long periods of time.
- It can waste a lot of our time and attention that could be used for more valuable activities.
