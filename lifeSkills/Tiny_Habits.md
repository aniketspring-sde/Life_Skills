## Question 2

How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.  
- B = MAP means Behavior = Motivation + Ability + Prompt.
- M = Motivation: means I should have a reason or desire to do the habit.
- A = Ability: means I should make the habit simple and easy to do.
- P = Prompt: means I should have a reminder or trigger that tells me to do the habit.
- Example: If I want to build a habit of studying Python, I can start by solving one small problem after I open my laptop. This makes the habit easy to start and repeat.
