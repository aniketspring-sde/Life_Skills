
## Question 1

What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?
- Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts.
- Don't interrupt the speaker; let them finish first, then respond.
- Focus on the speaker's sentences.
- Show interest in the speaker's topic.
- Show appropriate body language.
- During an important conversation, make notes.

## Question 2

According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?
- Try to understand the speaker's point of view.
- Notice the speaker's tone of voice, facial expressions, hand movements, and mood, then respond to that.
- Don't respond every time; take meaningful silence.

## Question 3

What are the obstacles in your listening process?
- Distracted by my own thoughts.
- Lack of focus on the speaker's thoughts.
- Thinking about a response without listening to the speaker.
